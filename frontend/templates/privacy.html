Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our priority

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

SafeNet AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered phishing detection service.

Key Principle: We collect minimal data necessary for service operation and security analysis. We do not track personal identity or sell user data to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 URLs Submitted for Analysis

When you use SafeNet AI to scan a URL, we collect and analyze:

  • The URL string you submit
  • Extracted features (domain characteristics, URL patterns, etc.)
  • WHOIS metadata (domain age, registration info)
  • Detection results and confidence scores
  • Timestamp of the scan

2.2 Account Information (If Registered)

If you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Hashed password (we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Account creation date
  • Last login timestamp

2.3 Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP address (for security and abuse prevention)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Session data

Important: We do NOT collect browsing history, personal identification documents, financial information, or any data beyond what is necessary for phishing detection.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Phishing Detection: Analyzing URLs to identify potential phishing threats
  • Service Improvement: Enhancing ML models and detection accuracy
  • Security: Preventing abuse, fraud, and malicious activity
  • User Dashboard: Displaying scan history and statistics to registered users
  • Research: Aggregated, anonymized data for academic research (no personal identification)

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Storage

Your data is stored in:

  • MongoDB database (for user accounts and scan history)
  • Secure servers with encryption at rest
  • Data retention: Scan history is retained for account holders; guest scans are not permanently stored

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

  • Password hashing using bcrypt
  • HTTPS encryption for data in transit
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. You acknowledge that you provide information at your own risk.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

5.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

SafeNet AI does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5.2 Limited Sharing

We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or government request
  • Security Threats: To prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymized, non-identifiable statistics for research

6. Cookies & Tracking

SafeNet AI uses minimal cookies for:

  • Session Management: Keeping you logged in
  • Theme Preference: Remembering dark/light mode selection

We do NOT use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt-Out: Stop using the service at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@safenetai.com.

8. Children's Privacy

SafeNet AI is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of SafeNet AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. International Users

SafeNet AI is operated from [Your Location]. If you access the service from outside this region, your data may be transferred to and processed in our servers. By using SafeNet AI, you consent to this transfer.
