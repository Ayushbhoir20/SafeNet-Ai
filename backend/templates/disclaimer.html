1. Educational & Security Assistance Purpose

SafeNet AI is designed and provided for:

  • Educational purposes: Learning about phishing detection, machine learning, and cybersecurity
  • Academic research: Supporting research projects, final year projects, and coursework
  • Security assistance: Helping users identify potentially malicious URLs
  • Awareness: Raising awareness about phishing threats and online security

SafeNet AI is NOT intended as a comprehensive enterprise security solution or a replacement for professional security services.

2. AI Predictions Are Probabilistic, Not Absolute

Critical Understanding: SafeNet AI uses artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms. All AI predictions are probabilistic in nature and represent likelihood, not certainty.

This means:

  • A URL classified as "Phishing" has a high probability of being malicious, but is not guaranteed to be phishing
  • A URL classified as "Safe" has a low probability of being malicious, but could still pose risks
  • A URL classified as "Suspicious" requires additional verification and caution
  • Confidence scores indicate the model's certainty, not absolute truth

False positives (legitimate sites flagged as phishing) and false negatives (phishing sites not detected) can and do occur.

3. Users Must Verify Critical Decisions Independently

You are solely responsible for verifying the safety of URLs before taking action. SafeNet AI provides analysis to assist your decision-making, but should not be the only factor.

Best Practices:

  • Do not rely solely on SafeNet AI for high-stakes security decisions
  • Verify suspicious URLs through multiple sources
  • Exercise caution when entering sensitive information on any website
  • Use additional security tools (antivirus, browser protection, etc.)
  • When in doubt, do not click or visit the URL

6. Limitations of Detection

SafeNet AI has inherent limitations:
